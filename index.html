HOW TO PLAY

⚡︎ OVERCLOCKING GLYPHS

Filled all your Glyph slots? Don't stop spending!

  • Duplicate Buy: When a Glyph you already own appears in the Shop, buy it to Level Up the one you are carrying, up to Level 3 (MAX).
  • Power Boost: Level 2 is half again as strong, Level 3 is double — no extra board slot needed.
  • Spot the Glow: Look for the [ II ] and [ MAX ] badges and the border glow on your board to see which gear is running hot.
1Tap or drag letter tiles from your hand up into the word line to build a word.
2Tap SUBMIT to play a valid word. Chips × Multiplier = the points you score.
3HANDS is how many words you can submit this stage — reach the target score before they run out.
4REROLL: mark up to 3 tiles, then confirm to swap them for brand new letters. SHUFFLE just reorders your current hand.

Glyph Colors
↺Green — Economy: extra hands, discards, tiles in your tray.
⚓Blue — Flat bonus points for vowels, consonants, rare letters.
⚔Red — MULT boosts for word length, patterns, repeats.
✦Purple — Wildcards, duplication, and other synergy tricks.
Glyph Order Matters
★Glyphs fire strictly left to right. Drag a glyph onto another slot, or tap one then tap a second, to swap their order.
★Put +MULT glyphs to the LEFT of xMULT glyphs. On 100 points: +15 then x3 = 4,800, but x3 then +15 = 1,800. Same glyphs, same word — order alone nearly triples it.
Italic Tiles

★Italic tiles are built to be kept, not played. Every Italic still sitting on your bench gives x1.5 MULT to the word you submit.
★Do NOT play these letters on the board unless necessary — holding them on your bench gives a 1.5x Multiplier to every played word!
