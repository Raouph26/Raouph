WORDSPYRE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

The short version: WORDSPYRE does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no advertising. Everything the game remembers is stored on your own device and never leaves it.

What the game stores on your device

WORDSPYRE saves a small amount of data locally so your progress and preferences survive closing the app. This is held in your device's own browser storage and is never uploaded anywhere.

  • Your run in progress — the stage, score, letters and glyphs you are carrying
  • Your overall progress — wins, unlocked glyphs, unlocked themes and personal bests
  • Your settings — music volume, sound volume, vibration and screen shake
  • Your chosen theme and colour palette
  • A short history of recent in-game events (the last 60), used only to fill in a bug report if you choose to send one

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so nothing survives that.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for a name, an email address or an account
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it does not collect any

Reporting a bug

The game has a Report a Bug button. Using it is entirely your choice. If you tap it, the game shows you a summary of your current game state — things like the stage you were on, your score, and which glyphs you had — and offers to open a Google Form in your browser so you can send it to us along with a description of what went wrong.

Nothing is sent unless you open that form and submit it yourself. You can see exactly what the summary contains before you send it, and you can copy it instead of sending it. If you do submit the form, your response is handled by Google Forms under Google's privacy policy.

Children

WORDSPYRE is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children specifically. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts doing something this page does not describe — for example if advertising or online leaderboards are added in a future version — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
