# Wordspyre — what to do, in order

Plain-language checklist. Do them in order. Don't skip ahead.

---

## STEP 1 — Put the game on the internet (20 minutes, free)

Right now the game is a file on your computer. Nobody else can play it and you
can't install it on your phone properly. This fixes that.

1. Download `wordspyre-website.zip` and unzip it. You get a folder with 7 files.
2. Go to **netlify.com/drop** in your browser.
3. Drag the whole folder onto the page.
4. Wait about 30 seconds.
5. It gives you a web address like `random-name-123.netlify.app`.

**That's it. Your game is live.** Anyone with that link can play it.

You'll be asked to make a free account to keep the link permanently. Do that.
You can rename the link to something like `wordspyre.netlify.app` in the site
settings.

✅ Done when: you can open that link on your phone and the game works.

---

## STEP 2 — Install it on your phone (2 minutes)

1. Open your link on your phone in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. **Android:** tap the three dots → "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
   **iPhone:** tap the share button → "Add to Home Screen".
3. It appears on your home screen with the tile icon.
4. Open it from there.

It should now be full-screen with no browser bars, and it works with no
internet.

✅ Done when: it opens from your home screen like a normal app.

---

## STEP 3 — Send it to your testers

Just send them the link and Step 2's instructions. No app store needed yet.
This is how you test properly before spending any money.

✅ Done when: your testers are playing from their home screens.

---

## STEP 4 — Google Play developer account ($25, one time)

Only do this when you're happy with how the game feels.

1. Go to **play.google.com/console** and sign up.
2. Pay the $25 one-off fee.
3. Google verifies your identity — this can take a few days, so start it early.

✅ Done when: you can see the Play Console dashboard.

---

## STEP 5 — Things Google will ask for (gather these before you start)

Google won't let you publish without these. Get them ready:

- **A privacy policy** — a web page explaining what data your game collects.
  It must be a real link. Ask me and I'll write the text for you; you paste it
  into a free page and send me the link.
- **Screenshots** — at least 2, taken on a phone. I can generate these for you.
- **A short description** (80 characters) and a **full description**.
- **A feature graphic** — a 1024x500 banner image. I can make this.
- **Content rating** — a questionnaire you fill in. Takes 10 minutes.
- **Data safety form** — you declare what data you collect. Straightforward
  once the privacy policy exists.

✅ Done when: you have all of these saved somewhere.

---

## STEP 6 — Turn the website into an app file (I do this with you)

Google needs an app file, not a website link. There's a tool that turns your
Netlify link into one automatically. Come back to me when you're at this step
and I'll walk you through it — it's about 30 minutes and mostly waiting.

---

## STEP 7 — Ads (money) — later

Don't do this until the game is live and people are playing. Ads need their own
account and approval, and they're pointless without players.

# Words I used that you didn't need

- "PWA" = a website that installs like an app. That's all it means.
- "HTTPS" = a secure web address. Netlify does this automatically, ignore it.
- "Analytics" = counting how people play so we know what to fix.
- "Service worker" = the bit that makes it work without internet. Already done.
