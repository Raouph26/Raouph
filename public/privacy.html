Thrum

Privacy Policy

Thrum does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no analytics of any kind.

What is stored, and where

The game saves a small amount of information in your device's local browser storage so it can remember where you were. It never leaves your device and is not readable by anyone else.

  • thrum.solved — which stages you have completed
  • thrum.theme — the theme you have chosen, if any
  • thrum.muted — whether sound is switched off
  • thrum.ads — counters used to pace advertisements

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it permanently. There is no server-side copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no way for us to restore it.

Network use

Every puzzle is generated on your device from its own number, so the game needs no connection to play. After the first load it works completely offline.

Advertising

This release does not serve advertisements and contains no advertising SDK. Should that change in a future version, this policy will be updated before the version is published, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.
